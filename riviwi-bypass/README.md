#### Riviwi.com Bypass — Instant & Direct Link Fast Skip

A lightweight, zero-latency automated bypass userscript for riviwi.com shorteners.

---

### Features

- **Instant Timer Skip:** Neutralizes countdown delays synchronously without waiting.
- **Full Navigation Automation:** Automatically clicks verification and destination buttons across both pages.
- **Popup & Tab-Under Guard:** Enforces direct tab navigation to prevent ad popups.
- **Lightweight & Fast:** Runs with `@grant none` and minimal overhead.

---

### Supported Domains

- `riviwi.com`
- `*.riviwi.com`
